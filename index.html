Cassie Photo Review

Cassie Photo Review is a private personal tool; it stores no data about other users.

It prepares and organises photo review files for its owner's personal use. Google Drive access is used to upload and manage files created by the tool.

Privacy policy

Privacy policy

This tool is for its owner's private use and is not a public service or an application for other users.

Google data access. It requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission to create and manage files used with the tool. It does not request access to all files in Google Drive.

Use and storage. Personal review files and related metadata are stored in owner-controlled storage and the owner's Google Drive. Authorization tokens are kept private and are not published on this website or in its source repository.

Sharing. Review files are shared only through access settings chosen by the owner. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models.

Control and deletion. The owner can revoke the tool's Google account access and delete its stored review files and local authorization data.

This website. This page contains no analytics, forms, advertising, or embedded third-party content. It does not receive photos, authorization tokens, or Google account data. GitHub Pages hosts the page and may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement.
